## Full Description

Every South African deserves to understand the law that governs their life.

Lexio is a free AI-powered legal assistant that answers your questions about South African law in plain, everyday language. No legal jargon. No expensive consultations. Just clear, accurate answers backed by the actual Acts of Parliament.

ASK ANY LEGAL QUESTION
Type your question the way you'd ask a friend. Lexio explains the law and cites the specific Act and section, so you can verify it yourself.

• "Can my boss fire me without a hearing?"
• "What are my rights if I'm arrested?"
• "Can my landlord increase rent without notice?"
• "What does POPIA mean for my personal data?"

IS THIS LEGAL?
Describe any situation — at work, at home, in the news — and Lexio analyses it against South African legislation. See which laws apply, whether any rules were broken, what your rights are, and what you can do about it.

KNOW YOUR RIGHTS
Browse a complete library of your rights organised by topic: workplace, consumer protection, housing, criminal law, family law, privacy (POPIA), and more. Each right is explained in plain language with the full legal citation.

SHARE WHAT YOU LEARN
Generate shareable rights cards for WhatsApp, Instagram, Twitter and Facebook. Help others know their rights too.

WHAT LEXIO COVERS
• Constitution and Bill of Rights (Chapter 2)
• Labour Relations Act (LRA) — unfair dismissal, CCMA, strikes
• Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA) — working hours, leave, wages
• Consumer Protection Act (CPA) — refunds, warranties, unfair practices
• POPIA — your data and privacy rights
• Criminal Procedure Act — your rights with police and during arrest
• Rental Housing Act — tenant and landlord rights
• Domestic Violence Act — protection orders
• Prevention of Illegal Eviction Act (PIE)
• And more being added regularly

TRUSTED INFORMATION
Every answer cites the specific Act, Section, and Subsection so you can verify the information yourself. Lexio provides legal information, not legal advice — for advice specific to your situation, consult a qualified legal professional.

FREE TO USE
Lexio is free with ads. Upgrade to Lexio Pro (R59/month or R499/year) for an ad-free experience, unlimited queries, document templates, and priority AI responses.
